# v3.00 Overview — Progression & Social Scalability

## Version Purpose

Give validated factory gameplay durable goals and carefully selected social context without making other players necessary.

## Primary Validation Question

> **Can players develop long-term goals and meaningfully compare or interact with other factories?**

## Scalability Objective

Support progression, collections, challenges, secure social reads, records, and optional shared goals.

## Player-Facing Objective

Players can pursue:

- machine collection
- research/specialization
- challenges
- factory milestones
- records
- factory showcases/visits
- friend comparison
- optional social goals

## Architecture Objective

Keep meta-progression separate from core simulation so factory changes do not destabilize long-term data.

Build read-safe representations for showcased factories rather than blindly loading another player's live state.

## Planned Phases

1. Meta-Progression Foundation
2. Collection, Research & Challenges
3. Factory Showcase & Visiting
4. Records, Leaderboards & Optional Social Goals
5. Long-Term Progression Validation

## Economy Considerations

Progression should unlock choices, not merely permanent global multipliers.

Prestige remains conditional. Trading remains excluded unless a later security/economy case justifies it.

## Security Risks

- leaderboard spoofing
- fake challenge completion
- unsafe loading of visitor data
- social reward farming
- progression duplication

## Performance Risks

- loading showcase layouts
- broader persistent state
- leaderboard calls
- challenge evaluation
- social UI load

## Deferred Systems

- unrestricted trading
- auction house
- mandatory co-op
- clans
- global market

## Exit Criteria

- long-term goals remain tied to factory play
- progression data is migration-safe
- showcase visits cannot modify owner data
- records are server-validated
- social features work with low population
- players engage with goals without reducing factory experimentation
